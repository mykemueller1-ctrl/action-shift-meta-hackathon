# Demonstration guide

Keep one workplace meal as the main story. Use the table and recovery examples only after the complete meal path is clear.

1. Introduce Myke Mueller and Never86’d. Start with the food question and the actual work that follows it.
2. Show the dated offer and the current menu. Keep expired information separate from current facts.
3. Read the fictional request. Show the headline quantity beside the item sum. Ask for clarification before quoting.
4. Prepare the integer price quote. Review price, timing and capacity for that exact version.
5. Show the two service windows and their separate simulated kitchen acknowledgments.
6. Add the authored guest change. Show the new request beside the historical commitment and require fresh approval.
7. Record the operator’s pickup only correction for this restaurant today. Show the next request retrieving it. Check another restaurant or date to show the boundary.
8. Show the source, decision and receipt together. State which steps were authored, simulated, recorded or actually run.

A saved Muse result must remain labeled as recorded. The earlier shift note replay cannot be presented as the new front desk’s live run. No real customer messages, payment records or private restaurant material belong in the public demonstration.
